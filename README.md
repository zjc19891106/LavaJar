# Lava Jar

Lava Jar is a paid iPhone game. This page is the support page for the App Store listing.

流心果罐是一款付费 iPhone 游戏。本页是 App Store 的支持页面。

## Support

The app has no account system. To report a bug or ask a question, open a GitHub Issue:

https://github.com/zjc19891106/LavaJar/issues

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

应用没有账号。反馈问题或提问请开一个 GitHub Issue，并写上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本，以及你期望发生的结果。

## What the game does

Drop soft fruit into a jar. Two of the same kind melt into one bigger fruit. Flick while moving to throw. Heat the jar, then shake it, when the pile gets tight. Cross the overflow line and the run ends.

把软水果滴进罐子。两个相同的会融成更大的一个。滑动中松手可以抛出。罐子变满时先加热，再摇晃。超过溢出线，这一局就结束。

## Purchases and ads

Lava Jar is a one-time purchase. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no accounts.

本应用为一次性买断。没有广告，没有订阅，也没有账号。

## Privacy

Lava Jar does not collect any information.

A high score and the sound and music switches may be stored on your device. That data stays on the device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

The same policy is available inside the app under Settings, then Privacy Policy.

《流心果罐》不收集任何信息。

最高分以及音效、音乐开关可能保存在你的设备上。这些数据只留在本机，不会发送给我们。删除应用后会一并清除。

同一份隐私政策也可以在应用内的「设置 → 隐私政策」中阅读。
